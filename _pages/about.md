I'm Mario Jauvin. I started this blog in December 2006. It covers computer and network performance, software development (especially Java), and other interests of mine, such as VoIP, open source projects and photography.

## What I write about

**Performance.** Measuring and visualizing how systems behave: [performance visualization](/tags/#visualization), notes from the CMG conferences ([San Diego 2007]({% post_url 2008-02-10-cmg-2007-in-san-diego %}), [Toronto 2008]({% post_url 2008-04-14-cmg-canada-in-toronto-april-15-16 %})), and [running an I/O benchmark with IOMeter]({% post_url 2016-03-07-running-io-benchmark-using-iometer %}).

**Java and software development.** Practical, working examples: [Spring multipart form handling]({% post_url 2012-02-23-handling-multipart-form-data-in-spring %}), a [JavaFX 11 + Spring Boot tutorial]({% post_url 2019-02-06-tutorial-on-how-to-write-and-run-javafx %}), and Maven tips, including [putting your WARs on a diet]({% post_url 2023-07-04-put-your-wars-on-diet-with-maven-maven %}) and some [useful Maven recipes]({% post_url 2023-08-02-useful-maven-recipes %}). Earlier posts look at [agile programming](/tags/#agile-programming) and Ruby on Rails.

**Networking and infrastructure.** VoIP, network protocols and open source projects, most recently [setting up a WireGuard VPN]({% post_url 2024-10-25-setting-up-wireguard-vpn-client-and %}).

**Photography.** My [digital photography workflow]({% post_url 2009-04-12-jauvin-digital-photography-workflow %}) and tips for [Adobe Bridge]({% post_url 2014-05-10-how-to-invoke-adobe-bridge-cc-photo %}).

**Everything else.** Occasional opinion pieces, for example on [reforming Canada's first past the post electoral system]({% post_url 2015-11-07-reforming-canadian-first-past-post %}). A few posts are in French or Spanish.
